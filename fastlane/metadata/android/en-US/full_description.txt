Remote Video Player plays videos stored on your computer or NAS over the home network — no transcoding server required.

<b>Sources</b>
<ul>
<li>SMB (Windows shares, Samba, Synology/QNAP), including custom ports</li>
<li>WebDAV and plain HTTP directory listings</li>
<li>Jellyfin: login, libraries, watched state</li>
</ul>

<b>Player</b>
<ul>
<li>Media3/ExoPlayer with gestures, audio/subtitle track selection, picture-in-picture and background audio</li>
<li>External subtitles (SRT/VTT/ASS) with per-file delay</li>
<li>Resume positions, "Continue watching", up-next for series</li>
</ul>

<b>Library</b>
<ul>
<li>Combined library and fast search across all servers</li>
<li>Series grouping with seasons, episodes and watched marks</li>
<li>Optional TMDB posters and details (needs your own TMDB API key)</li>
<li>Offline downloads</li>
</ul>

<b>Casting</b>: DLNA/UPnP TVs through a local proxy. The F-Droid build does not include Google Cast (Chromecast), which requires proprietary Google Play services.

Android TV is supported (launcher banner, remote control).

Passwords are encrypted with the Android Keystore. No ads, no tracking. Source code: Apache-2.0.
